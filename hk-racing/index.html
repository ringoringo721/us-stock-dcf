Probability engine

From factors to P(win)

1. Score

Each runner gets a linear ability score: weighted sum of scaled features (rating, 狀態, 騎師, draw, pace, going…).

2. Softmax

Win probability is softmax of scores. Higher temperature flattens the board; lower sharpens favourites. Field always sums to 100%.

3. Edge

Market-implied probability is taken from win odds (normalized). Edge = model % − market %. Positive means the model likes it more than the tote.

4. Next

Fit weights on historical HKJC results (LightGBM / logistic), add place/quinella via finishing-order simulation, and blend lightly with the market for calibration.
